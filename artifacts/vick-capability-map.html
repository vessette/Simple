Vick Capability Map

Every capability planned for Vick, the AI sales agent, laid out along the customer's journey from first contact to renewal. Each tile shows its status, its autonomy ceiling and whether the decision always stays with a person. Select a tile to see its full specification.

Under each title, ten small bars are the autonomy ladder, R0 to R9. The dark bar marks the first-release rung, lighter bars run up to the ceiling, and the hatched bar is R8, which the platform can't reach. Every capability sits at R0 today.

Why a ceiling, not a target

The research sets the highest rung each capability could ever reach. It's a limit, and it stays a draft until the sales manager reviews it. A capability climbs one rung at a time, approved on evidence.

Why so many stop below R7

Anything that sends a message, moves money or overwrites a person's judgement stays human. The agent prepares, explains and flags; a person sends, charges and decides.

Why the manager queue comes first

Every promotion needs the manager to see and approve the agent's work. Until the decision queue exists in the platform, every other capability stays a draft.

Specifications come from the capability stories in the project repository (github.com/vessette/Simple). Ceilings are drafts. People are referred to by role.
