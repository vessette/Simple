# Offer routing: three decisions for you

**For:** Roselyn · **From:** Victoria · **For the weekly check-in**

## Why now

The system decides which offer each lead gets, and that also puts the lead into a campaign.

Right now it **can**:
- put a word-of-mouth lead into **cold** outreach on its own;
- enroll the same person in **two campaigns at once**.

There's no way for you to override it, or to see who decided. That's the over-pursuing of cold leads you said we can't do. These three decisions stop it.

*Whether this has already happened to real leads isn't known yet. The system allows it, and Ali or Kes can check whether it has.*

## The three decisions

| # | Decision | My suggestion | The trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Should the system keep re-checking leads (every 6 hours) and changing their offer, or pause that until it can be overridden? | **Pause it**, with Ali | Offers stop changing on their own. New leads still get a first offer. |
| 2 | Should picking an offer also start outreach? | **No: label only.** You or your team start outreach. | Slower first contact, but nobody gets the wrong message. |
| 3 | What makes a lead "warm"? | **Your list of signals**, for example a form, an idea or a call, with a time limit | Needs your list. The system only counts an advisory session today. |

These are suggestions. Change any of them.
